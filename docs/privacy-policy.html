Yaseen Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Yaseen uses local settings, location and public content to provide prayer times, nearby mosques, Quran reading and reminders. There is no Yaseen account or server, analytics, advertising or sale of personal data.

Location and maps

OS location is optional. Manual mode protects your selected location from GPS changes. Background location is off by default and requires your explicit opt-in and OS authorization. Force-quit, battery restrictions and the operating system can prevent background updates. Saved coordinates are rounded to four decimal places. Apple Maps on iOS, Google Maps on Android and OS geocoding services may process your coordinates and connection information under their own policies; Yaseen does not operate those services.

Public content requests

Tanzil Arabic text, QuranEnc Rowwad English translation, QuranEnc As-Sa'di tafsir and the Amiri Arabic typeface are included with the app. Factual chapter metadata and Mushaf line positions are requested through jsDelivr. Recitations and recording-specific verse timings come from MP3Quran. Mosque schedules may be requested from a mosque's public website. Requests identify the requested page, reciter, surah or public resource; saved coordinates and learning records are not attached to Quran content requests. Providers receive your IP address and normal connection information, and their policies may allow server logs and aggregate usage statistics. The two bundled Adhan clips require no download. Full content notices, edition versions and source links are available in Settings. Offline audio uses full-surah recordings and verse intervals; a size estimate and confirmation are shown before a passage download.

Local data and deletion

Settings, saved locations, Quran reading position/bookmarks and progress are kept on the device. Date-keyed trackers are pruned to a 90-day window; the separate memorization history is not silently pruned. Optional learner preferences, prior-material reports, mistake notes and external teacher references also remain local. A teacher reference entered by a user is not independently verified by Yaseen. Memorization practice does not record a microphone, upload recitation or automatically assess proficiency. Progress export/import is user-controlled and excludes Quran text, fonts and recordings; exported files may contain your private notes, so store and share them carefully. Verified JSON, font and Quran-audio caches have size budgets. Clear Downloaded Content deletes caches and passage downloads while preserving settings, reading and memorization history. Reset All Local Data disables location updates, cancels reminders and clears app data; a failure is shown if the OS cancellation cannot complete. Restart after clearing to unload content still in memory. Reset Today's Progress affects today's checklists only.

Android backups are disabled and the iOS AsyncStorage settings/learning store is excluded from backup. Document-directory passage downloads may be included in operating-system-managed backups. Local progress is not guaranteed to survive uninstall or device loss; you can explicitly export a progress backup. Previously created device backups are managed through the operating system. Yaseen has no remote user-data copy to delete. OS services and content providers apply their own retention policies.

Notifications

Prayer and Dhikr notifications are scheduled locally. Notification settings are not sent to a Yaseen server. The operating system controls delivery, DND, silent-mode behavior and exact-alarm access.

Children and changes

Yaseen has no user accounts or analytics and does not knowingly collect children's information on a Yaseen server. This policy will be updated if these practices change.
