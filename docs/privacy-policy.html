Privacy Policy

Yaseen | ياسين · Last updated: July 6, 2026

Yaseen ("the app") is built to work almost entirely on your device. This policy explains the little data the app touches, why, and where it goes.

Location

The app asks for your location to calculate accurate prayer times for where you are and to find nearby mosques. Your coordinates are stored only on your device and used solely to run these calculations. Yaseen does not operate any server that receives, stores, or processes your location — there is no account, sign-in, or backend of any kind.

Maps

The Nearby Mosques map is rendered using Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps (Android). Displaying the map may share your approximate location with Apple or Google in order to draw the map — this is governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies, not this app's.

Place names

When you set or search a location, the app may ask your device's operating system to translate coordinates into a place name (e.g. "Vancouver, BC"). This uses Apple's or Google's on-device/location services, subject to their respective privacy policies — Yaseen itself never sees or stores this lookup.

Quran, Adhan audio & mosque content

Surah text, tafsir, Mushaf page images, and Adhan audio are downloaded from public content sources (e.g. jsDelivr, everyayah.com) by page or surah number only — no personal or location data is sent with these requests. Some mosque prayer-time cards may fetch the mosque's own public website to show live times; again, no personal data is attached to that request.

Notifications

Prayer and Adhkar reminders are scheduled entirely on your device using your local settings. Nothing about your notification preferences leaves your phone.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in required, and none exists.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No data leaves your device except the map, place-name, and public-content lookups described above.

Your data, your device

All your settings, saved locations, and progress (prayer checklist, Adhkar, Quran bookmarks) are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using "Reset Today's Progress" in Settings, removes this data. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is no remote copy to request or delete.

Children's privacy

Yaseen does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it collects no personal data on any server in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
